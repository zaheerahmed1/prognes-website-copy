# AI AGENCY WEBSITE — MASTER SPECIFICATION

Version: 1.0
Status: Design / Strategy Source of Truth

---

# 01 — CORE BRAND

## Core Brand Idea

Unfair Operational Leverage.

The agency exists to give businesses an operational advantage through intelligent automation, AI-powered growth systems, and agentic infrastructure.

The central idea:

There will always be companies manually grinding for growth and companies operating through automated intelligence.

We build the latter.

---

# 02 — AGENCY POSITIONING

We are an AI systems and growth agency.

The business has two connected pillars:

1. AI Growth
2. AI Systems

These are NOT separate businesses.

They form one operating loop:

Growth creates demand.

Systems convert and process that demand.

Automation compounds the result.

---

# 03 — AI GROWTH

AI Growth is a core agency capability.

It must be prominently represented on the website.

AI Growth includes:

- AI UGC
- AI product photography
- CGI product advertising
- Creative production
- Paid advertising
- Performance marketing

## AI UGC

AI UGC is NOT an optional side service.

It must have clear representation in the website architecture and visual storytelling.

The website should communicate that AI can accelerate:

- Creative production
- UGC-style content
- Product demonstrations
- Ad variations
- Campaign creative
- Testing velocity

Do not hide AI UGC inside a generic "AI services" list.

---

# 04 — AI SYSTEMS

AI Systems is the second major pillar.

Capabilities include:

- AI automation
- Agentic AI
- AI employees
- AI sales systems
- Lead qualification
- CRM automation
- Customer support automation
- AI chatbots
- AI voice agents
- Internal business automation
- Workflow automation
- RAG / knowledge systems

The website should present these as interconnected business systems rather than a random list of tools.

---

# 05 — BUSINESS MODEL

The agency's value proposition is not:

"we use AI."

The value proposition is:

"We engineer systems that create operational leverage."

Technology is the mechanism.

Business outcomes are the value.

The website should therefore avoid excessive technical jargon in the primary marketing narrative.

The business has complementary capabilities.

Founder / technical side:

- AI automation

# 10 — HERO

The hero is the first major statement of the company.

It should communicate the central idea of:

UNFAIR OPERATIONAL LEVERAGE

The hero should contain:

- Eyebrow / small label
- Large headline
- Supporting copy
- Primary CTA
- Secondary CTA or supporting navigation
- Signature visual / 3D treatment

The visual should represent intelligence becoming infrastructure.

The hero 3D should be abstract, geometric, architectural, and sophisticated.

It must not look like generic AI artwork.

---

# 38 — 3D PERFORMANCE

Live 3D must be treated as progressive enhancement.

Capability ladder:

1. Mobile / reduced motion / low capability
   → static image or video

2. Tablet / mid capability
   → simplified visual or video

3. Desktop / capable GPU
   → live 3D

Maximum one major WebGL canvas per page.

3D must be lazy-loaded.

Pause rendering when offscreen.

Use compressed models and optimized textures.

# 45 — ACCESSIBILITY

Target:

WCAG 2.2 AA.

Requirements:

- Semantic HTML
- Correct heading hierarchy
- Keyboard navigation
- Visible focus
- Skip link
- Accessible navigation
- Reduced motion
- Text alternatives for informational visuals
- Decorative canvas marked appropriately
- Adequate contrast
- Accessible forms

Motion must never be required to understand the content.

---

# 46 — PERFORMANCE

Performance is a design requirement.

Priorities:

1. Fast first paint
2. Fast LCP
3. Low CLS
4. Good INP
5. Minimal JS
6. Lazy media
7. Optimized images
8. Deferred 3D
9. Mobile-first performance

Do not sacrifice performance for visual effects.
